# Market Preference & Operating Rules

## Primary Rule: Indian Markets Only (NSE / BSE)
- The user operates exclusively in the **Indian Stock Market** (NSE / BSE).
- All stock recommendations, screeners, analysis, alerts, market regimes, and trade signals MUST target Indian tickers (e.g., symbols with `.NS` for National Stock Exchange or `.BO` for Bombay Stock Exchange, or standard NSE tickers).
- Never recommend US tickers (e.g., AAPL, NVDA, TSLA, SPY, QQQ) unless explicitly asked by the user for international comparison.
- Trading hours, holidays, market indices (NIFTY 50 `^NSEI`, BANKNIFTY `^NSEBANK`, SENSEX `^BSESN`), and currency (INR `₹`) must reflect Indian market conventions.
- Never make blind architectural changes. Clarify any ambiguities with the user before altering core pipelines.
